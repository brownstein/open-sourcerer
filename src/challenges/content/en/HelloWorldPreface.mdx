# Preface
Greetings Sourcerer! Today we're going to learn to log output to the console,
making your input appear on screen.

This is important to your programming journey because console logging is
the simplest way to undestand the state of larger programs - you'll be
logging frequently to determine the values of variables and the state of your
programs' control flow.
