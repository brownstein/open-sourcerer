# Introduction to Open Sourcerer

Welcome to **Ecma 6**, an ever-shifting battleground where spellcasters channel elemental forces through code. In Open Sourcerer you do more than wield pre-made spells—you craft them. Every function, loop, and conditional you write translates into a powerful move on the field.

This introductory page anchors three pillars of the experience:

1. **Narrative context** – why you’re here and who you’re fighting for.
2. **Gameplay mindset** – how scripting, experimentation, and iteration power your success.
3. **Learning roadmap** – what to read next to transform curiosity into mastery.

## Lore Snapshot

You are the **Sourcerer**, a champion tasked with stabilizing the Ecma 6. The world is fractured by elemental unrest—Fire leaps unpredictably, Ice drifts into forbidden territories, and Spark surges across the sky. Your guide, **Adana**, mentors you through trials that blend tactical combat with programming puzzles.

As you progress, your spells unlock new combos, elemental synergies, and reactive behaviors. Each challenge teaches you both narrative lessons and coding techniques.

## A Beginner’s First Steps

1. **Complete the initial tutorial** with Adana to learn movement, the spell console, and scanning.
2. **Open the Docs tab** from the dock to explore the sections outlined in the overview.
3. **Experiment in the Code Editor** – paste examples from the docs, run them, and check the console for output.
4. **Iterate quickly** – tiny changes (e.g., adjusting timing or element blend) help you see cause and effect.

Remember: mistakes are part of the journey. Open Sourcerer encourages trial, failure, observation, and refinement—the same loop professional spellcrafting developers follow.
